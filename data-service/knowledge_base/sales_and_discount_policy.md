# Sales and Discount Policy

Document owner: Chief Revenue Officer · Version 4.1 · Effective 1 April 2026

## 1. Pricing
All products are sold at the list price in the product catalogue unless a discount is approved under this policy. Prices are in Indian Rupees (INR) and include GST unless stated otherwise.

## 2. Discount approval limits
| Discount on an order line | Who can approve |
|---|---|
| Up to 10% | Sales representative |
| Above 10% and up to 20% | Regional Sales Manager |
| Above 20% and up to 35% | Head of Sales |
| Above 35% and up to 50% | Chief Revenue Officer, with written justification |

No discount above **50%** may be given under any circumstances. Systems reject order lines with a discount above 50%.

## 3. Volume pricing for business customers
- SMB customers ordering 5 or more units of the same product receive an automatic 5% volume discount.
- Enterprise customers ordering 10 or more units of the same product receive an automatic 8% volume discount.
- Volume discounts count towards the approval limits above.

## 4. Festive season campaigns
During the Diwali and year-end season (October to December), marketing may run campaigns of up to 15% on Accessories, Audio and Networking without additional approval. Laptops and Smartphones campaigns require Head of Sales approval because of their low margins.

## 5. Sales targets and commission
Sales representatives are measured on delivered and shipped revenue in their region. Cancelled and returned orders are excluded from revenue and from commission. Commission is 2% of revenue up to target and 3% above target.

## 6. Credit terms
- Retail customers pay at the time of order.
- SMB customers may receive 15-day credit after a credit check.
- Enterprise customers with an annual contract receive 30-day credit.
