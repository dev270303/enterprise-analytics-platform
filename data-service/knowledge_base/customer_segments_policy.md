# Customer Segments and Service Policy

Document owner: Marketing & Customer Success · Version 1.4 · Effective 1 January 2026

## 1. Business segments
Every customer belongs to exactly one business segment, recorded at sign-up:
- **Retail**: individual consumers buying for personal use.
- **SMB**: small and medium businesses with up to 250 employees, buying for their own use.
- **Enterprise**: large organisations with more than 250 employees, usually under an annual contract.

## 2. Service entitlements by segment
| Entitlement | Retail | SMB | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Return window | 10 days | 15 days | 15 days |
| Credit terms | None | 15 days | 30 days |
| Dedicated account manager | No | From ₹25 L annual spend | Yes |
| Support priority floor | LOW | MEDIUM | HIGH |
| Free on-site installation | No | Monitors and furniture | All hardware |

The **support priority floor** means a ticket from that segment can never be logged below that priority.

## 3. Behavioural (RFM) segments
The analytics team also scores customers monthly with an RFM model (recency, frequency, monetary value) using K-Means clustering:
- **Champions**: bought recently, buy often, spend the most. Invite them to the loyalty programme and early access.
- **Promising**: recent buyers with lower spend so far. Nurture them with onboarding offers.
- **At Risk**: valuable customers who have not bought for a long time. The account manager must contact them within 14 days.
- **Hibernating**: low spend and inactive. Include them in quarterly win-back campaigns only.

## 4. Churn risk
A customer is **HIGH churn risk** when the days since their last order exceed three times their usual gap between orders (or 180 days for one-time buyers). HIGH-risk Enterprise and SMB customers are reviewed every week by Customer Success.

## 5. Data protection
Customer contact details are restricted to the CRM. Analytics and AI tools use only anonymised or business-level fields.
