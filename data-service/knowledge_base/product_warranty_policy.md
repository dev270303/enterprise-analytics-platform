# Product Warranty Policy

Document owner: Product Management · Version 2.3 · Effective 1 April 2026

## 1. Standard warranty periods
| Category | Standard warranty | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Laptops | 1 year (2 years for Elite tier) | Battery covered for 6 months |
| Smartphones | 1 year | Screen damage not covered |
| Monitors | 3 years | Includes dead-pixel replacement in the first 30 days |
| Audio | 1 year | |
| Networking | 2 years | |
| Accessories | 6 months | |
| Office Furniture | 5 years on frame, 1 year on mechanisms and upholstery | |
| Software | Not applicable | Covered by the licence agreement and updates |

## 2. Extended warranty
Customers can buy an extended warranty at the time of purchase:
- +1 year for 8% of the product price
- +2 years for 14% of the product price
Extended warranty is available for Laptops, Smartphones, Monitors and Networking only.

## 3. What is not covered
Physical damage, liquid damage, unauthorised repairs, normal wear of consumable parts, and software issues caused by third-party applications.

## 4. Claim process
1. The customer raises a support ticket in the **Product Defect** category with the invoice and serial number.
2. The technical desk diagnoses remotely within the SLA for the ticket's priority.
3. If a repair is needed, pickup is arranged within 3 working days in metro cities and 5 working days elsewhere.
4. Repairs are completed within 10 working days. If a product cannot be repaired within 15 working days, it is replaced.

## 5. Dead on arrival
Products that fail within 7 days of delivery are replaced immediately without diagnosis, in line with the Refund and Returns Policy.
