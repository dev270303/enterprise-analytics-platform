# Customer Support SLA Policy

Document owner: Head of Customer Support · Version 2.0 · Effective 1 January 2026

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how quickly customer support tickets must be resolved, how tickets are prioritised, and when they must be escalated.

## 2. Priority levels and resolution targets
| Priority | Typical examples | Resolution SLA |
|---|---|---|
| CRITICAL | Business-down issue for an Enterprise customer, payment taken but order missing, data/security concern | **6 hours** |
| HIGH | Wrong or damaged product delivered, billing error over ₹10,000, delivery delayed more than 3 days | **24 hours** |
| MEDIUM | Delivery delayed up to 3 days, invoice corrections, return pickup scheduling | **48 hours** |
| LOW | General questions, account changes, feedback | **72 hours** |

The SLA clock starts when the ticket is opened and stops when it is marked resolved. A ticket **breaches its SLA** if it is resolved later than its target, or is still open after the target has passed.

## 3. Ticket categories
Tickets are classified as Delivery, Billing, Product Defect, Returns or Account. Category drives routing: Delivery and Returns go to the logistics desk, Billing to the finance desk, Product Defect to the technical desk, and Account to the customer care desk.

## 4. Escalation matrix
- At **50% of the SLA** elapsed with no first response, the ticket is escalated to the team lead.
- At **SLA breach**, the ticket is escalated to the Support Manager for the region, and the customer receives a proactive update.
- CRITICAL tickets are escalated to the Head of Customer Support immediately when opened.

## 5. Service-level targets for the team
- At least **85%** of tickets in each category must be resolved within SLA every month.
- The **90th percentile** resolution time must stay below 1.5 times the SLA target for each priority.
- Each agent should keep no more than 25 open tickets at a time.

## 6. Reporting
The support dashboard reports the SLA breach rate by category, mean and 90th percentile resolution time, and an agent leaderboard ranked by SLA compliance. A daily insights report is e-mailed to management listing new SLA breaches.
