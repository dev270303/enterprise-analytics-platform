# Refund and Returns Policy

Document owner: Customer Operations · Version 3.2 · Effective 1 April 2026

## 1. Scope
This policy covers refunds, returns and replacements for every order placed through the Online, Store and Partner channels in all four regions (North, South, East and West).

## 2. Return window
- Retail customers may return products within **10 days** of delivery.
- SMB and Enterprise customers may return products within **15 days** of delivery.
- Software licences can be returned only if the licence key has **not been activated**.
- Custom-configured laptops and made-to-order office furniture cannot be returned unless they are defective.

## 3. Condition of returned goods
Products must be returned with original packaging, accessories, manuals and invoice. Opened but undamaged items are accepted, but a restocking fee may apply (section 5).

## 4. Refund timelines
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **7 working days** after the returned item passes quality inspection.
- Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) within 7 working days of receiving the customer's bank details.
- Partner-channel orders are refunded through the partner, who must pass the refund on within 10 working days.

## 5. High-value orders (above ₹50,000)
Orders with an invoice value **above ₹50,000** follow additional controls:
- Every refund or return above ₹50,000 requires **approval from the Regional Sales Manager** before it is processed.
- A **physical inspection** by the logistics team is mandatory before any refund is issued.
- Opened, non-defective items in orders above ₹50,000 attract a **restocking fee of 10%** of the item value (capped at ₹25,000 per order).
- The refund is processed within **10 working days** after approval, instead of the standard 7.
- Enterprise customers with an active annual contract are exempt from the restocking fee.

## 6. Defective and damaged products
Products that are dead on arrival or damaged in transit are replaced or refunded in full, with no restocking fee. The customer must report the issue within **48 hours** of delivery, with photos, by raising a support ticket in the Product Defect or Delivery category.

## 7. Cancellations
Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are shipped. After shipment, the cancellation is treated as a return.

## 8. Fraud prevention
More than three returns by the same customer in 90 days triggers a manual review by the Finance team before further refunds are approved.
